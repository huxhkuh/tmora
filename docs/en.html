A few things
worth knowing.

Where is my data stored?+

On your computer, in the Windows app. Work data is not sent to a server. Export a full backup from Backup & settings and keep another copy outside your computer.

Can I work offline?+

Yes. After installation, track time, manage tasks and review reports without a connection. Downloads and update checks require internet.

How do updates work?+

In the installed app: Backup & settings → Check for updates. You choose when to download and install. The portable version does not update itself.

What is supported?+

Windows 10 and 11 on x64 processors. The app supports English and Hebrew; time uses the Israel time zone and currency is ILS (₪). Mac, Linux and Windows ARM have not been verified. The installer remains in Hebrew.

Is it really free?+

Yes. No subscription, no sign-up and no paid AI service. The application’s source code is available under the MIT license.

What about the Windows warning?+

The files do not yet have a publisher certificate. Use the official links on this page; there is no need to change Windows security settings. Integrity checksums are available on the release page.
